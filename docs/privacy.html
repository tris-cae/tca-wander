Privacy Policy

Notice Explore · Last updated 9 August 2026

The short version: Notice Explore stores your saved places on your phone and nowhere else. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. Nothing you save is sold or shared.

What the app stores

Everything you save — place names, categories, your personal notes, addresses, and coordinates — is written to a database on your device. It is not uploaded to any server, and it is not backed up to us. If you delete the app, that data is gone.

Your settings (notification radius, distance units) are also stored locally on the device.

Location

Notice Explore requests location access for three purposes:

  • Showing where you are on the map
  • Saving a place at your current position
  • Alerting you when you come near a place you saved

Proximity alerts need background location access ("Always Allow"), because the app has to notice you are nearby even when it is closed. Your position is compared against your saved places on the device. Your location history is never transmitted or recorded anywhere.

You can revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings. The rest of the app continues to work; only proximity alerts stop.

Notifications

Proximity alerts are local notifications generated on your device. They are not push notifications sent from a server, and no third party is involved in delivering them. Each place can alert you at most once per 24 hours.

When data leaves your device

There are exactly two features that send anything off your phone, and both only run when you actively use them:

  • Place and address search. What you type into the search fields is sent to the Google Places API to return matching results. This is governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • AI itinerary generation. If you ask the app to build a route, the names, categories, coordinates, and any notes for the relevant saved places are sent to a server we operate (Cloudflare Workers), which forwards them to Anthropic's Claude API to plan the route. No name, email, device ID, or account identifier is attached to these requests, because the app has no such information. The request is not stored on our server after the response is returned.

If you never use search or route generation, no data leaves your device at all.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts and no sign-in
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising and no ad networks
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing of personal information

Children

Notice Explore is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your control over your data

Because everything lives on your device, you are in full control. You can delete an individual place from the List tab, revoke location or notification permission in iOS Settings, or delete the app to remove all of its data at once. There is no server-side copy for us to delete on your behalf.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is handled, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
